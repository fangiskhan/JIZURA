Lyrics

  • One line = one phrase. A blank line adds a short pause
  • Hold the light/until morning … / marks where a cut breaks
  • *glass* … emphasis (bigger, higher-impact treatment)
  • A trailing ! … adds a flash and a shake
  • word|ruby or note … small text for annotation layouts
  • [01:23.45]Lyric … uses the LRC timestamp as-is
  • Lines starting with # … comments (ignored)
